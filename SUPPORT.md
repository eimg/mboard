# MBoard support

For help with MBoard on iPhone or iPad, email [eimg@fairwayweb.com](mailto:eimg@fairwayweb.com). Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the app where the issue appeared. A short example using invented text is usually enough.

## Enable the keyboard

1. Open **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard** and choose **MBoard**.
2. Open the MBoard practice app and tap the writing field. If you use a hardware keyboard on iPad, show the onscreen keyboard first.
3. Hold the system globe and select **MBoard**. Use the far-left key on the bottom row. Tap **abc** for English or **ကခ** for Burmese.

MBoard works with Full Access off. The system keyboard appears in password fields, some phone-number fields, and apps that restrict third-party keyboards.

## Manage suggestions

Long-press a learned suggestion and choose **Forget This Suggestion**, or open the keyboard menu and choose **Clear Learned Words → Clear for Both Languages**. The menu opens from the MBoard logo in 0.1.1 or the settings gear in the submitted 0.1 build. Turn off **Word Suggestions** to hide candidates and pause new learning. [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)
