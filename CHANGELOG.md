# Changelog

## 0.1.1 — in development

- Choose when English capitals appear in mixed Burmese text. The Auto-Capitalization panel shows examples and saves each choice as you tap. The blue `abc` or `ကခ` key switches languages.
- The keyboard now follows the system appearance. The logo and hide button have more clearance from the rounded top corners. Tap the logo for typing settings and Typing Tips. Tips follow the active language: Burmese explains hold-and-release alternatives and stacking with ္; English explains cursor dragging and double-Space punctuation. Back and Hide Keyboard stay above the scrolling tips. The typing toolbar also includes Hide Keyboard.
- Emoji search returns to typing when you tap the app’s input, including its existing cursor position, or change its selection. The search bar replaces the typing toolbar. Its left arrow returns to the emoji list, and Done resumes typing.
- The full emoji list now puts saved recents and 144 everyday emoji first. Updated Unicode data and English/Burmese keywords make common searches such as “happy”, “thanks”, “love” and “lol” useful, with newer emoji shown on supported iOS versions.
- Choose the quoted spelling to keep exactly what you typed. English capitalization respects manual Shift, and immediate Backspace lets you retry an automatic capital in lowercase.
- Starting an English word after Burmese, or a Burmese word after English, now adds a space automatically.
- Hold a Burmese key and slide to type a stacked consonant, such as က္က on က or န္ဒ on န. Lifting in place selects the familiar Gboard shortcut.
- English suggestions now put the top choice in the center and offer spelling corrections while typing or editing a word. Tap to correct a typo, add a missing space or restore an apostrophe, such as `Im` → `I'm`. Pressing Space keeps your spelling.
- English learns frequently typed words and uses the preceding word to rank matches locally. Sensitive fields skip suggestions and learning. Forget individual words or clear the history in keyboard settings.
- After accepting an English suggestion, keep typing to start the next word or tap punctuation to attach it directly.
- Hold Space and drag to move the caret. Double-tap English Shift for Caps Lock.

## 0.1 — awaiting App Store review

Submitted as build 11. The initial version includes offline English and Burmese typing, a practice app and support for iOS and iPadOS 16 or later.

- Switch languages in the keyboard. Type Burmese Unicode using the Gboard-style layout, familiar vowel ordering and long-press alternates.
- Complete words using the on-device English dictionary and a bundled Burmese word list. Selected suggestions can move up in future matches.
- Use automatic English capitalization, including English words in Burmese sentences, or turn it off in keyboard settings.
- Browse emoji in one grid, search in either language and hold an emoji for skin-tone choices.
- Choose System, Light or Dark appearance. Core typing works with Full Access off.
